# Inputs required from Parallel

## Organization and ownership

- Confirm the OpenAI organization that will own the plugin.
- Confirm the exact verified publisher/developer name.
- Ensure an operator has Apps Management write access.
- Complete individual or business identity verification.

## Domain and support

- Provide an owner who can publish the portal-generated verification token at
  `https://search.parallel.ai/.well-known/openai-apps-challenge`, or confirm an
  allowed parent-domain location.
- Provide the final public support URL.
- Confirm that the website, privacy policy, and terms URLs in `listing.md` are
  the approved URLs for this submission.

## Brand and distribution

- Provide the approved logo files requested by the portal.
- Choose the countries or regions where the plugin should be available.
- Confirm `Parallel Web Systems` as the public developer name, or provide the
  exact replacement.

## Privacy and policy

- Have the product, security, and legal owners review `data-practices.md`.
- Provide confirmed retention, logging, service-improvement/model-training,
  subprocessors, and deletion-request details.
- Assign an authorized owner to complete the portal policy attestations.

## Review evidence

- Record and host the demo using `demo-script.md`, then provide its public URL.
- Confirm the five positive and three negative test cases reflect intended
  production behavior.
- Confirm whether anonymous, rate-limited access is the only launch mode. No
  reviewer credentials are needed if it is.
